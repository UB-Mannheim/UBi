# Corporate and Financial Databases

This page provides an overview of key corporate and financial databases available at the University of Mannheim, including access information, content descriptions, and support resources.

## Overview: Where to Find Which Data

| Data Type | Databases |
| --- | --- |
| Analyst forecasts | I/B/E/S (via LSEG Workspace) |
| Mutual funds | CRSP Mutual Funds, Institutional (13F) Holdings, Mutual Fund Holdings (via CRSP, LSEG Workspace) |
| Accounting data | Orbis, COMPUSTAT North America, COMPUSTAT Global, LSEG Workspace |
| Banks and financial service industry | BankFocus, SNL Unlimited (S&P Global) |
| M&A and ownership structure | Aktienführer Data Archive, Orbis M&A, SDC Worldwide M&A |
| Executive information | Orbis, Aktienführer Data Archive, Dun & Bradstreet Hoovers, COMPUSTAT Execucomp |
| Corporate filings | FinancialFilings |

## Database Descriptions and Access

### Wharton Research Data Services (WRDS)

Many financial databases are accessible via the web-based WRDS interface.

- [Access and university specific information](https://dbis.ur.de/UBMAN/resources/8646)
- [WRDS Website](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/)

### Aktienführer Data Archive

- **Contents:** Historic German financial market and corporate data (1870–2018), including standardized reports from German and foreign corporations traded at German stock exchanges. Reports include information on head offices, management, operations, investments, shareholder structure, and income statements.
- **Available history:** 1956–2016 (Database), 1870–2016 (digitised full-text PDFs)
- **Access:** [Free access](https://digi.bib.uni-mannheim.de/aktienf%C3%BChrer/data/index.php) within the University of Mannheim IP range

### BankFocus (Bureau van Dijk and Moody's Analytics)

- **Contents:** Standardized financial statements, regulatory information, ownership data, and financial ratios for over 46,700 banks worldwide.
- **Help:** [BankFocus user guide](https://help.bvdinfo.com/mergedProjects/131_EN/Home.htm)
- **Access:** [BankFocus](https://dbis.ur.de/UBMAN/resources/101865) (Bureau van Dijk interface), via [WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### COMPUSTAT Execucomp (Standard & Poor's)

- **Contents:** Executive compensation data from annual proxy statements (DEF14A SEC form), including salary, bonus, stock awards, incentive plans, pensions, and more.
- **Help:** Documentation available via WRDS.
- **Access:** [via WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### COMPUSTAT Global (Standard & Poor's)

- **Contents:** Financial and market data for publicly traded companies in over 80 countries, with up to 12 years of annual history.
- **Help:** Documentation available via WRDS.
- **Access:** [via WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### COMPUSTAT North America (Standard & Poor's)

- **Contents:** U.S. and Canadian company fundamentals and market information for over 30,000 companies, including indices, industry segments, banks, market prices, dividends, and earnings.
- **Help:** Documentation available via WRDS.
- **Access:** [via WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### CRSP (Center for Research in Security Prices)

- **Contents:** Security prices, returns, and volume data for NYSE, AMEX, and Nasdaq; includes stock indices, portfolio data, treasury bond and risk-free rates, and mutual fund databases.
- **Help:** Data description and definition guide via WRDS.
- **Access:** [via WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### Dun & Bradstreet Hoovers

- **Contents:** Over 220 million business records from 30,000 sources in 190 countries, including company data, sector, employee numbers, risk classes, and online signals.
- **Access:** [Dun & Bradstreet Hoovers](https://dbis.ur.de/UBMAN/resources/105992)

### FinancialFilings

- **Contents:** International database of corporate reports and regulatory disclosures, with over 28 million documents from 53,000 companies in 48 markets and 21 languages. Includes annual, quarterly, ESG, and sustainability reports, as well as other filings. Data is available in PDF, Markdown, and JSON, and linked to identifiers such as ISIN, LEI, and ticker symbols. Paid API and bulk downloads are available for research.
- **Access:** Authenticate via [FinancialFilings](https://financialfilings.com/) using University of Mannheim credentials.

### I/B/E/S

- **Contents:** Consensus and detailed forecasts from security analysts, including earnings per share, revenue, cash flow, growth projections, and stock recommendations.
- **Help:** Documentation available via WRDS.
- **Access:** [via WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### Institutional (13F) Holdings

- **Contents:** Institutional Common Stock Holdings and Transactions as reported on SEC Form 13F, covering managers with $100 million+ in assets.
- **Help:** Documentation available via WRDS.
- **Access:** [via WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### LSEG Workspace (including Datastream and Worldscope)

- **Contents:** Comprehensive company and market data, including financial indicators, stock market, bonds, money and foreign exchange markets, and commodities. Data is available for download.
- **Help:**
  - [LSEG Finance Essentials](https://www.lseg.com/en/training/learning-centre/certification/lsegfe-certification)
  - [LSEG Workspace tutorials and courses](https://www.lseg.com/en/training/learning-centre/)
  - [Excel Add-In tutorial](https://www.lseg.com/content/lseg/en_us/training/learning-centre/learning-centre-catalogue/video-details.html?id=getting-started-with-refinitiv-workspace-excel-add-in&playlistId=1_6rg1cjpw)
  - [Datastream datatype description (PDF)](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/media/Lehrstuehle/bwl/Maug/Database_info/Datastream_dataypes.pdf)
- **Access:**
  - Access is limited to University of Mannheim students and staff.
  - Short-term use: Terminal at Schneckhof Library information desk, coordinated via ILIAS group “[Financial and statistical databases](https://ilias.uni-mannheim.de/ilias.php?baseClass=ilrepositorygui&ref_id=1227320)”.
  - Frequent use: [Request a personal account](https://www.bib.uni-mannheim.de/medien/zugang-zum-lseg-workspace/). Staff and doctoral students: semester access; students: one month.
  - For access questions, contact the subject librarian for business administration.

### Mutual Fund Holdings

- **Contents:** Security holding information for all registered mutual funds reporting to the SEC, plus 3,000 global funds. Includes NYSE/AMEX/NASDAQ, Toronto, and Montreal stocks.
- **Help:** Documentation available via WRDS.
- **Access:** [via WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### Orbis (Moody's)

- **Contents:** Standardized information on over 500 million public and private companies worldwide, with strengths in private company data. Includes financial, ownership, governance, and industry information.
- **Help:** [Orbis user guide](https://help.bvdinfo.com/mergedProjects/68_EN/Home.htm)
- **Access:** [Orbis](https://dbis.ur.de/UBMAN/resources/12630) (Moody's interface)
- **Extended Access:** Professors, academic staff, and supervised student theses can request bulk downloads and historical data. Contact orbis@uni-mannheim.de for credentials (administered by the Dean's Office of the Business School).

### Orbis M&A (formerly Zephyr)

- **Contents:** Over 2.6 million global M&A and related deals, including IPOs, joint ventures, private equity, and rumors. Detailed deal, company, ownership, and financial data.
- **Help:** [Zephyr user guide](https://help.bvdinfo.com/mergedProjects/83_EN/Home.htm)
- **Access:** [Orbis M&A](https://dbis.ur.de/UBMAN/resources/7703) (Bureau van Dijk interface)

### SDC Worldwide Mergers, Acquisitions & Alliances (Thomson Financial)

- **Contents:** Data on worldwide mergers, acquisitions, alliances, and stock repurchases. Covers approximately 672,000 global M&As from 1985 to present (U.S. data since 1979).
- **Help:** Documentation available via WRDS.
- **Access:** [via WRDS](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/login/) (registration required)

### SNL Unlimited (S&P Global)

- **Contents:** Financial data, news, and analyses for over 6,500 public and 50,000 private companies in banking (worldwide), insurance (USA, Canada, Europe), and financial services (USA, Canada).
- **Help:** After login, click the “Help” button in the database header.
- **Access:** Restricted to University of Mannheim members; personal registration with university email required via [S&P Global registration page](https://platform.spgi.spglobal.com/).

## Further Information
